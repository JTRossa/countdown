How to play

Six rounds, the same puzzle for everyone each day. Each round has a 30 second clock.

Letters rounds (3)

  • Make the longest word you can from the nine letters. Each letter can be used once.
  • Tap the tiles or type, then press Declare. One point per letter; a nine-letter word scores 18.
  • If the clock runs out, a valid word left in your slots still counts.

Numbers rounds (2)

  • Use + − × ÷ on the six numbers to reach the target. Each number can be used once, and you don't have to use them all.
  • Only whole, positive results are allowed. Tap a number, an operation, then another number.
  • Exact: 10 points. Within 5: 7 points. Within 10: 5 points. Your closest number is declared automatically.

Conundrum (1)

  • Unscramble the nine letters into a single word. 10 points. You can guess as often as you like before time is up.

Maximum score: 84.
